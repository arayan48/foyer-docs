**FiveM is not supported by Foyer yet.** It appears in the app marked “Bientôt” (coming soon). This page helps you prepare the machine in the meantime.

## Before you start

- **A licence key**, free, to create on the [Cfx.re portal](https://portal.cfx.re) with your Cfx.re account.
- **A legitimate copy of GTA V** for the players.

## Player limit

The number of slots depends on Cfx.re, not on the machine. By default, a server accepts **48 players** with OneSync Infinity. An Element Club subscription goes further, up to 2048 slots depending on the plan. Current tiers are on the [official page](https://fivem.net/server-hosting).

## How many players?

FiveM mostly depends on the number and quality of the scripts. With the same number of players, a loaded RP server needs far more than an empty one.

| Players | Profile | Cores | Server RAM |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 20 | Empty server or few scripts | 2 to 4 | 4 to 8 GB |
| 20 to 32 | Some scripts (jobs, garages) | 4 to 6 | 8 to 12 GB |
| 32 to 48 | Standard ESX or QBCore RP | 6 to 8 | 12 to 16 GB |
| 48 to 64 | Large RP (phone, inventory, MDT) | 8, high clock speed | 16 to 24 GB |

Figures adapted from the guides of two hosting companies, [ElypseCloud](https://wiki.elypsecloud.com/en/fivem/ram-cpu-players/) and [Flux](https://runonflux.com/games/fivem/fivem-server-requirements). They are estimates, not measurements.

Add **1 to 2 GB** for the MariaDB database with ESX or QBCore.

**Example: 2 cores and 4 GB.** Enough for a test server or about ten players without a framework. Not enough for ESX or QBCore RP: aim for at least 4 cores and 8 GB.

## What uses resources

- **Scripts running in loops.** Spot them with the in-game `resmon` command or the txAdmin monitor.
- **Added vehicles and maps.** They weigh on the disk, player downloads and memory.
- **The database.** Badly indexed queries slow down the whole server.
