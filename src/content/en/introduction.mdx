Foyer installs, runs and monitors your game servers from a desktop app. It currently handles **Minecraft (Paper)** and **FiveM (txAdmin)**. The app interface is in French for now.

## How it works

Foyer is made of two programs:

- **The agent** runs on the machine that hosts the games. It downloads the servers, starts them, restarts them after a crash and, on Windows, opens their ports on the local network. It listens on port `7878` over HTTPS.
- **The app** connects to an agent with an address and a token. It shows the dashboard, live consoles and the settings of each server.

Both can run on the same machine, or the app on your PC and the agent on a server on your local network.

## Where to start

1. Check that your machine is suitable: [Minimum requirements](/configuration/prerequis).
2. Install the agent: [Windows](/installation/serveur-windows) or [Linux](/installation/serveur-linux).
3. Install the app on your PC: [App](/installation/client).
4. Connect: [First connection](/installation/premiere-connexion).
5. Choose the RAM for your servers: [Minecraft](/serveurs/minecraft), [FiveM](/serveurs/fivem).
